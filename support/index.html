About Funergy+

Funergy+ is the internal business app for the staff of Funergy Group Inc. It is used for store operations, shifts, tips, purchasing, accounting, human resources and internal messages. It is available as an iPhone app and on the web at funergy-plus.com.

Who can use it

Staff with an account set up by the company. There is no public sign-up.

Signing in

  1. Choose your location (a store or Office).
  2. Choose your name.
  3. Enter your PIN.

Some management and office staff sign in with their email and password, or with a passkey. On a shared store device, choose “Log in as this store”.

You can switch between Japanese and English on the sign-in screen.

Changing your PIN

After signing in, open My Page and choose “Set PIN”.

If you cannot sign in

If you forgot your PIN or cannot sign in, please contact us using the information above.
