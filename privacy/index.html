Funergy+ is an internal business app used by the staff of Funergy Group ([要確認: 運営者の正式名称と所在地], “we”) to run our restaurants and head office. This policy explains what information the Funergy+ iPhone app and the web version at funergy-plus.com collect and store, and how that information is used.

1. Who uses Funergy+

Funergy+ is for our staff. Accounts are set up by the company; the app has no public sign-up. Information about job applicants and new hires is also handled in the app, as described below.

2. Information we collect and store

Sign-in and security

  • Your name (chosen on the sign-in screen) and your PIN. PINs are checked by our server.
  • For some management and office accounts: email address and password, handled by our authentication provider.
  • Passkeys and registered devices: the public key, a device name, and when the device was registered and last used. The private key stays on your device.
  • Sign-in codes and invitation links sent to your email address.
  • Sign-in records: time, result, IP address, and browser or device information (user agent).

Staff profile

  • Name, employee code, role, grade and title, assigned and support stores, employment type and status.
  • Dates such as hire date, first work day, leave periods and resignation date.
  • Email address and phone number.
  • Date of birth, the last 4 digits of a company card, medical and dental insurance enrollment, and the company-paid insurance premium.
  • Pay information: hourly wage or monthly salary and related pay settings.
  • Your name as it appears in our point-of-sale system (Toast).

New hire onboarding

When a new hire completes onboarding through an invitation link, we collect: name, address, phone number, email address, date of birth, Social Security number, visa or work authorization details, images of identity documents (passport, driver’s license, Social Security card), signature, agreement to company policies, and I-9 checklist status. These items are sent to a separate server function and are not included in the app’s regular data sync.

Hiring

Managers record job applicants: name, store and position applied for, phone number, email address, how they applied, application date, notes, work history, skills, and the file name of a résumé.

Work and business records

  • Shifts and schedules; attendance (clock-in and clock-out), sales, payment and tip data imported from Toast; labor hours and labor costs.
  • Cash tips and tip distribution.
  • Sales, budgets and financial reports; inventory, purchasing, recipes and costs.
  • Invoices and receipts, including photos and PDF files.
  • Expense and vacation requests, mileage requests (including the starting point, destination and distance of each trip), work requests, and their approvals.
  • Daily operations checklists, daily reports and photos attached to them; meeting materials and goals.
  • Employee discount use: who used it, grade, store and time.

Evaluations and development

Career Score evaluations, growth records (including grade and pay history), mid-period reviews, development plans and training progress, and bonus evaluations and calculations.

Messages

Direct messages, group messages and discussions about requests, including mentions, read status and attachments (photos and PDF files). Messages are stored on our server and are not end-to-end encrypted.

Notifications

If you turn on notifications in a supported browser, we store the notification address that your browser provides, linked to your staff ID. Notifications only say that there is a new message; they do not include the sender, the conversation name or the message text.

Device information and change history

  • A random device identifier created by the app and kept on your device, and your device type (computer, iPhone, iPad or Android) determined from the user agent.
  • A change history of who changed which data and when (staff ID, name, role, device identifier and device type).

Information stored on your device

To work quickly and when the connection is poor, the app keeps copies of the data you can access, and your settings, in the storage of the app or browser on your device.

Reviews of our restaurants

Reviews of our restaurants on Google (the reviewer’s display name, rating, comment and dates) are imported so that our team can follow up on feedback.

What Funergy+ does not use

Funergy+ does not request your device location, does not access your contacts, and does not include advertising or third-party analytics trackers. The camera and photo library are used only when you choose to take or attach a photo. [要確認: iPhone アプリの本体（ネイティブ部分）に追加の SDK が無いこと]

3. How we use information

  • To run daily restaurant and office operations: shifts, attendance, tips, purchasing, inventory, accounting and budgets.
  • For human resources: hiring, onboarding, evaluations, training, and pay and bonus calculations.
  • For internal communication and notifications.
  • For security: verifying sign-ins, limiting access by role, and keeping sign-in and change records.

4. Who can see information

Within the company, access is limited by role and store. Sensitive items such as individual pay, date of birth and insurance are shown only to authorized roles, and shared store devices have a limited view.

5. Service providers and external services

Funergy+ uses the following services to operate:

  • Supabase — database, sign-in, file storage and server functions for the app’s data.
  • Toast — our point-of-sale system. Attendance, sales, payment and tip data are read from Toast.
  • Google — Gmail (sending sign-in codes and invitations; receiving and forwarding invoices), Google Drive (storing invoice files), Google Business Profile and Places (reviews and ratings of our restaurants), and Google Maps (address search and distance for mileage requests).
  • Anthropic — invoice documents are sent to the Claude API so that their contents can be read automatically.
  • Intuit QuickBooks — invoice files are forwarded by email to our accounting system.
  • LINE — business alerts, which may include staff names and work details, are sent to company LINE groups.
  • QR code service (api.qrserver.com) — an onboarding invitation link is sent to this service to create a QR code image.
  • Open-Meteo — store coordinates are sent to get weather forecasts. No personal information is sent.
  • jsDelivr and cdnjs — app libraries and icons are downloaded from these networks, which receive standard request information such as your IP address.
  • Browser push services (for example Apple or Google) — deliver notifications if you turn them on.
